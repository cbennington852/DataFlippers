## Educational Philosophy
As AI increasingly permeates various aspects of modern life, students and individuals lacking understanding for complex AI models fall prey to corporate propaganda, fear, and a dangerous over-reliance. Understanding how a system works makes it less scary, and promotes true understanding.  AI literacy empowers individuals to critically evaluate these systems, fostering informed decision-making and promoting responsible technological usage.  Moreover, a basic grasp of AI models can demystify complex technologies, enabling students to navigate a world shaped by intelligent systems with greater confidence and agency. 

The goal of DataFlippers is to provide free, open source, and fun software with the goal of promoting AI literacy.
