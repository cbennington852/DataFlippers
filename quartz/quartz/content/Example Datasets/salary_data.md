A dataset featuring salary data, as well as the number of years of experience, and other related data.

### Dataset Structure
| Attribute             | Type        | Description                                                                   |
| :-------------------- | :---------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Age**               | Numerical   | The chronological age of the employee in years.                               |
| **Experience**        | Numerical   | Total number of years of professional work experience.                        |
| **Job Role**          | Categorical | The specific title or position held by the employee (e.g., Manager, Analyst). |
| **Education Level**   | Categorical | The highest level of formal education attained (e.g., Bachelor's, Master's).  |
| **Salary**            | Numerical   | The annual gross compensation of the employee.                                |
