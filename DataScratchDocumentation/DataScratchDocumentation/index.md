## Educational Philosophy

As AI increasingly permeates various aspects of modern life understanding its underlying principles becomes essential. AI literacy empowers individuals to critically evaluate these systems, fostering informed decision-making and promoting responsible technological development.  Moreover, a basic grasp of AI models can demystify complex technologies, enabling students to navigate a world shaped by intelligent systems with greater confidence and agency.

DataScratch is free to use and open to everyone. I believe AI literacy shouldn't be hidden behind a paywall. Everyone should be free to learn.
